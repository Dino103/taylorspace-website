Modern Low-Poly Cow

A low-poly cow decoration designed to print without supports — left unpainted so every one can be finished to match a different real-life cow.

Image 1 placeholder
Image 2 placeholder
Image 3 placeholder
Image 4 placeholder
Image 5 placeholder
Image 6 placeholder
Image 7 placeholder

Design Intent

Made as a companion piece to the low-poly chicken below, after a family member wanted more farm-themed decor. Most cow models online are posed standing, which prints poorly without supports — this one is based on a seated reference model that already avoided that problem, then reworked into a low-poly style.

Print Settings

Printed at 100% scale with 2 walls and 5% cubic infill — light settings suited to a display piece rather than something load-bearing. Fine to increase wall count or infill for a heavier feel.

Files

Free to download on Printables — STL file, no supports required.

View on Printables

Notes

Left unpainted on purpose — every cow's markings are different, so the model is meant to be painted in-slicer or by hand to match whatever look you're going for.

Modern Low-Poly Chicken

A support-free, low-poly chicken decoration with a two-color comb and wattle for straightforward multicolor printing.

Image 1 placeholder
Image 2 placeholder
Image 3 placeholder

Design Intent

Started as a search for chicken decor that didn't need supports or an awkward print orientation — most models online required one or the other. Reworked from a nesting-chicken reference model that already printed clean, adapted into a low-poly form.

Print Settings

Printed at 300% scale with 2 perimeters and 8% infill. The comb and wattle are separate color regions, easy to assign a second color to in-slicer for a two-tone print.

Files

Free to download on Printables — STL file, no supports required.

View on Printables

Notes

No supports needed at any tested scale — one of the more consistently easy prints in the lineup.

Modern Low-Poly Christus

Design Intent

Reimagines the traditionally all-white Christus statue with color — the robes finished in red, a detail drawn from imagery in the Book of Isaiah. Meant as a modern take on a classic piece rather than a straight replica.

Print Settings

Printed at three sizes — 4", 6", and 8" — with a slower outer-layer speed for a cleaner surface finish. Small dual-color prints can blob on undercalibrated printers and may need light post-processing.

JHT3D · Functional · Original Design

Tie Buddy

A thin TPU clip worn behind the shirt buttons that keeps a necktie in place against wind and movement.

Image 1 placeholder
Image 2 placeholder
Image 3 placeholder
Image 4 placeholder

Design Intent

Built to replace store-bought silicone tie clips that deformed and wore out quickly. Sized to fit most dress shirts — works down to about 2.7 in / 67 mm between buttons, with 3.6 in / 91 mm as the max spacing before the fit gets loose.

Print Settings

Printed in TPU for flexibility and durability. The part is only about 1 mm thick, so it sits invisibly under a shirt without adding bulk — and thin enough that a lot fit on one print bed at once.

Files

Free to download on Printables — STL file.

View on Printables

Notes

Quick to batch-print given how thin it is — makes an easy gift for anyone who wears a tie regularly.
